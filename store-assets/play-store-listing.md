## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Master the NATO phonetic alphabet with Alpha Bravo Quiz — the fast, focused way to learn Alfa, Bravo, Charlie and all 26 code words used by pilots, military, emergency services, and professionals worldwide.

Whether you're a student pilot studying for your radio exam, a military recruit, an emergency dispatcher, or just someone who wants to spell things clearly over the phone, Alpha Bravo Quiz helps you build instant recall through active practice.

HOW IT WORKS

Quiz Mode — Test your knowledge with multiple-choice questions. Choose random letters, focus on your weak spots, or tackle the full A-Z alphabet in one session.

Flashcards — Study NATO code words at your own pace. Flip through all 26 letters with their phonetic equivalents.

Morse Code — Learn the morse code patterns for each letter. Identify letters from dot-dash sequences.

Spelling Practice — Spell out words using the NATO alphabet. Build real-world fluency for radio communication.

Daily Challenge — A fresh set of 10 questions every day. Keep your streak alive and track your consistency.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS

- Per-letter accuracy tracking shows exactly which letters you know and which need work
- 7-day activity chart visualizes your study habits
- Current and longest streak tracking keeps you motivated
- Detailed session history and statistics

EARN ACHIEVEMENTS

Unlock badges as you hit milestones — from your first quiz to mastering every letter. Achievements give you goals to work toward and celebrate your progress.

FEATURES

- 6 study and practice modes
- Smart "weak letters" mode targets your trouble spots
- Haptic feedback for correct and incorrect answers
- Sound effects (toggleable)
- Daily reminder notifications to maintain your streak
- Beautiful dark theme designed for comfortable studying
- Works completely offline — no internet required
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no account required
- All data stored locally on your device

Alpha Bravo Quiz is free, private, and built for focused learning. Download it and start mastering the NATO phonetic alphabet today.
