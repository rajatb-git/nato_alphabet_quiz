# Privacy Policy — Alpha Bravo

**Last updated:** March 18, 2026

## Overview

Alpha Bravo ("the App") is a NATO phonetic alphabet quiz app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

## Data Collection

**Alpha Bravo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All data generated by the App — including quiz scores, statistics, achievements, and settings — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. No data is sent to any server, third party, or analytics service.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally:

- **Quiz statistics**: per-letter accuracy, number of attempts, and session history
- **Achievements**: which badges you have unlocked
- **Settings**: your preferences for haptic feedback, sound effects, and notifications
- **Daily challenge progress**: your current streak and daily completion status

This data never leaves your device. You can delete all stored data at any time from the Settings screen within the App, or by uninstalling the App.

## Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

- **Notifications**: To send optional daily reminder notifications. You can enable or disable this in the App's settings. No data is transmitted — notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
- **Vibration**: To provide haptic feedback during quizzes. This can be toggled off in settings.

## Third-Party Services

Alpha Bravo does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for all ages.
